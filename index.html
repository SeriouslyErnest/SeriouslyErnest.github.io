Live Singapore public transport

BusFlash

See the buses you care about across nearby stops at a glance.

BusFlash started as a simple class exercise and became something I kept refining for my own commute. I have more than one nearby bus stop, so the useful question is not only “when is the next bus?” but “which way should I walk?”

The interface focuses on a small set of chosen stops and services, using clear visual cues so the decision can be made quickly without digging through a full transport app.

About

I like turning fuzzy problems into something concrete.

My day-to-day work is not software engineering. These projects are a way for me to explore product ideas, requirements, workflows and user experience by taking them far enough to become working applications.

AI-assisted development has lowered the barrier between an idea and a usable prototype. What interests me most is deciding what should be built, how it should work, what should stay simple, and whether the result actually solves the original problem.
