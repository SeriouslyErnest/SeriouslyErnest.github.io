BusGlance

See the buses you care about across nearby stops at a glance.

busglance started as a simple class exercise and became something I kept refining for my own commute. I have more than one nearby bus stop, so the useful question is not only “when is the next bus?” but “which way should I walk?”

The interface focuses on a small set of chosen stops and services, using clear visual cues so the decision can be made quickly without digging through a full transport app.

Closed beta Household utility

HomeStock

Know what you have. Buy what you need.

HomeStock is a household inventory and restocking tool designed to reduce duplicate purchases and make it easier to know what is running low before heading to the shops.

The workflow is built around everyday household behaviour: scan items when they come in, deduct them when they are used, set minimum stock levels and let low-stock items feed into a shared shopping list.

Currently running as a closed beta while I continue refining the workflows and usability.

About

I like turning fuzzy problems into something concrete.

My day-to-day work is not software engineering. These projects are a way for me to explore product ideas, requirements, workflows and user experience by taking them far enough to become working applications.

AI-assisted development has lowered the barrier between an idea and a usable prototype. What interests me most is deciding what should be built, how it should work, what should stay simple, and whether the result actually solves the original problem.
